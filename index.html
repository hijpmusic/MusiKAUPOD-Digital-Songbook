ABOUT MUSIKAUPOD

MUSIKAUPOD DIGITAL SONGBOOK

A digital musical companion of the Holy Infant Jesus Parish Music Ministry Community (HIJP-MMC).

ABOUT THE APP

MusiKaupod is an offline-capable digital songbook developed to make the musical resources of the Holy Infant Jesus Parish Music Ministry Community (HIJP-MMC) more accessible, organized, and convenient to use.

Designed for choir members, cantors, instrumentalists, accompanists, and other music ministry volunteers, the app brings together liturgical songs, service songs, musical scores, lyrics, and audio tonal guides in one digital platform.

MusiKaupod aims to support the preparation, practice, and celebration of liturgical music by providing convenient access to the musical materials needed by the ministry. Whether during rehearsals, liturgical celebrations, or personal practice, members can use the app as a readily available musical reference.

Built as an installable Progressive Web App (PWA), MusiKaupod is designed to work offline after its resources have been downloaded, making its contents accessible even without a continuous internet connection.

FEATURES AND CAPABILITIES

  • Digital Song Library – Organized collections of liturgical and service songs for convenient browsing and reference.
  • Lyrics and Song References – Access to available song lyrics and other musical information.
  • Musical Notation – PDF musical scores, including available SATB arrangements, for choir members and instrumentalists.
  • Audio Tonal Guides – Listen to available audio references to help learn melodies and individual musical parts.
  • Smart Search – Find songs conveniently through song titles and, where supported, lyrics.
  • Songs by Liturgical Season – Musical collections organized according to the seasons of the Church, helping music ministers prepare appropriate songs for liturgical celebrations.
  • Offline Accessibility – Access downloaded app resources without requiring a continuous internet connection.
  • Cross-Platform Support – Designed for smartphones, tablets, and desktop computers through a compatible web browser.

Changelog & Version History

Version 1.0.3 — 06 November 2026 · Critical UI Restoration

  • Restored the MusiKanam game and Catholic Music Doctrines screens and their menu entries.
  • Restored player, mini-player, calendar, and module initialization wiring.
  • Added a dedicated MusiKalendaryo menu entry and removed a stray visible text artifact.

Version 1.0.2 — 06 November 2026 · Prayer and MusiKanam Update

  • Added five traditional prayers in English, Latin, Tagalog, Cebuano, Bikol, and Spanish.
  • Added the supplied Minasbate versions of Ama Namon, Maglipay Ka Maria, and Pag-omaw sa Santisima Trinidad; left the Apostles’ Creed and Hail Holy Queen pending the parish-approved translations.
  • Expanded MusiKaaraman and Katesismo 101 to 120 questions each, and Misa Master and LiturgiColor to 100 questions each.
  • Temporarily disabled OneChant; retained the dynamic 20-question LiriKaupod game.
  • Corrected MMC Directory profile Back navigation and refreshed the offline cache version.

Version 1.0 — Official Stable Release

  • Introduced the complete About page and acknowledgments.
  • Added the Music Ministry Directory with council, choir, and accompanist sections.
  • Retained the existing songbook, search, player, lineup, calendar, and offline capabilities.

Beta 2.0 — Search and Update Improvements

  • Improved song search with a centered search dialog and optional lyric searching.
  • Added typo-tolerant suggestions and refined search presentation.
  • Improved update detection and service-worker update handling.
